# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS, no build step. Served by GitHub Pages from `studiographic/studiographic-site` at studiographic.ca (www redirects to the apex).

## Users

Two visitors, served equally: people who found one of Studiographic's apps or games (MacMin first) and want to see what else exists, and clients or employers looking at the work with hiring or collaboration in mind.

## Product Purpose

The home page for Studiographic, a one-person studio that makes small apps and games. It says who this is, lists the projects with a link to each, and gives a way to get in touch. Success: a visitor knows within seconds what Studiographic is, reaches a project, or finds the contact.

## Operating Context

A landing page, not a portfolio. Projects grow over time; each one has its own site (MacMin lives at macmin.studiographic.ca), so this page links out rather than showcasing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Project list starts with one entry: MacMin, minimize animations for macOS, at https://macmin.studiographic.ca. More apps and games are added later, so the list must read well with one entry and with many.
- Contact: hello@studiographic.ca and GitHub (https://github.com/studiographic).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Studiographic.
- Logo: wordmark "Studiographic" in white, the "o" a circle filled half white, half blue (`/Users/m22stewa/Documents/studiographic/studiographic-logo.png`, cropped copy at `logo.png`). Shown on black.
- User's brief for this page: nothing fancy, super clean, minimalistic; typography is important.

## Evidence on Hand

- The logo PNG (no vector version yet).
- MacMin and its site. No testimonials, clients, press or other projects exist yet; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Restraint is the brand: every element on the page earns its place.
2. Type does the work that imagery would do elsewhere.
3. Built to grow: adding a project is adding one entry, not redesigning.
4. Each project speaks on its own site; this page points to it.
